# Ebb Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 3, 2026_

Ebb is built to stay out of your way, and that includes your data. **Ebb doesn't collect, store, sell or share any personal information.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer can't see anything you do in Ebb.

## What stays on your iPhone

Everything you set up in Ebb is stored only on your device: the apps you add, your widgets, colors and wallpaper, mindful pause settings, focus and blocking schedules, and Insights (app opens, apps you let go and the reasons you give). Insights history is kept for 30 days and then deleted. If you delete Ebb, all of this is deleted with it.

## Apple services Ebb uses

Some features use Apple's own services. Ebb only uses them for the feature described, and the information goes to Apple, not to the developer.

- **Screen Time (Family Controls).** Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time API. Apple keeps the apps you choose private: Ebb only receives anonymous tokens and never learns which apps they are or how you use them.
- **Location and Apple Weather.** If you add the Weather widget and allow location access, your location is sent to Apple Weather to get the forecast. Ebb doesn't store or share it. You can pick a place instead of using your location.
- **App Store search.** When you search for an app to add, or Ebb fetches an app's icon, it looks the app up in Apple's public App Store directory. Only the app's name or ID is sent.
- **Photos.** Ebb asks to *add* to your photo library so it can save matching wallpapers. It can't see your photos.
- **In-app purchases.** Ebb Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and Ebb only learns whether Plus has been bought.

Apple's handling of this information is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

Ebb doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
